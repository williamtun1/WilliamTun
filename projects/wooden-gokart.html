PERSONAL PROJECT • VEHICLE DESIGN

Three-Wheel Wooden Go-Kart

Designed and built a three-wheel wooden go-kart for under $300, with a target top speed of approximately 40 mph.

OVERVIEW

Designing a low-cost vehicle from scratch.

This project began as a challenge to build a functional vehicle using inexpensive materials and simple manufacturing methods. The goal was to create a lightweight three-wheel go-kart while learning more about chassis design, steering geometry, drivetrain integration, braking, and structural loading.

The wooden chassis was designed around commercially available components to keep the total build cost below $300. Throughout the project, I iterated on component placement and packaging to improve stability, maintainability, and overall vehicle performance.

DESIGN & BUILD

Simple materials, practical engineering.

The chassis was constructed primarily from wood, which kept the project inexpensive and easy to modify during development. I designed the vehicle around the drivetrain, steering system, wheels, brakes, and driver packaging.

Building the kart required balancing structural strength, weight, cost, and manufacturability while making sure the vehicle remained serviceable as the design evolved.
