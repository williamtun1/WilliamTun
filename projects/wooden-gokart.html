PERSONAL PROJECT • VEHICLE DESIGN

Three-Wheel Wooden Go-Kart

Designed and built a low-cost three-wheel go-kart from scratch, integrating the chassis, steering, drivetrain, braking system, and driver packaging into a functional vehicle.

OVERVIEW

Building a vehicle from scratch.

I designed and built this three-wheel go-kart as a personal project focused on learning vehicle design through hands-on fabrication.

The goal was to create a functional vehicle on a limited budget while working through real engineering constraints including chassis stiffness, steering geometry, drivetrain alignment, braking, packaging, and weight distribution.

DESIGN & BUILD

Simple materials. Practical engineering.

The chassis was constructed primarily from dimensional lumber, keeping the vehicle inexpensive, easy to manufacture, and simple to modify throughout development.

I designed the chassis around the engine, chain drivetrain, steering system, wheels, braking components, and driver position while considering structural strength and component accessibility.

The ability to quickly modify the wooden chassis allowed the design to evolve as problems were discovered during assembly and testing.

TESTING

From a concept to a functioning vehicle.

After assembling the drivetrain and steering system, the go-kart was tested as a complete vehicle. Testing helped identify areas that needed adjustment and provided experience troubleshooting a mechanical system outside of a controlled CAD environment.
